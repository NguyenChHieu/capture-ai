# Privacy policy (beta)

Capture Organizer stores content **you explicitly save** (text, links, images you upload). We do not read Messenger, Instagram, or other apps in the background.

## Data we process

- Account identifiers (OAuth subject or email from your identity provider).
- Captures and organized items you create.
- Optional screenshots/images you upload (stored in object storage).

## AI subprocessors

When AI processing is enabled, capture text may be sent to configured LLM providers (e.g. OpenAI) under their API terms. We use API settings aimed at **no training on your content**. You can disable AI in self-hosted deployments.

## Your rights

- **Export:** `GET /v1/me/export`
- **Delete account:** `DELETE /v1/me` (cascades captures and items)

## Retention

Data is kept until you delete it or close your account.
